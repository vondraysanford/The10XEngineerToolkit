# Outage Update

```text
Write a stakeholder status update for an outage that continues.

Audience: <<<for example, non-technical leadership and the affected internal
teams>>>

Rules:
- Structure: the current status (user-visible impact, in plain language) /
  progress since the last update / our current actions / the next update at
  <time>.
- State the impact in user terms ("customers cannot check out"), never in
  system terms ("the pods are in a crash loop").
- Do not speculate about the root cause. Do not assign blame. Write "under
  investigation" until the team confirms the cause. A wrong early theory in
  writing stays on record permanently.
- Include a specific time for the next update, even if the news is "no
  change". If you give no updates, readers think that the situation is out of
  control.
- Three true sentences are better than ten sentences of reassurance. Do not
  write "we take this seriously".

Current facts (times, what is confirmed, what the team tries now):
<<<paste the raw facts and the timeline>>>
```
