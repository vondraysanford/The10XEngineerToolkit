# Tech Debt Proposal

```text
Turn my engineering complaint into a business case to reduce this debt.

Rules:
- Frame the case in business outcomes: incidents, slower delivery, onboarding
  cost, and security or EOL risk. Use numbers from my notes where possible
  ("this area caused 4 of the last 6 incidents", "every feature here costs
  +2 days").
- Structure: the cost of no action / the proposal (the smallest increment
  that helps, not the full ideal refactor) / the cost of the work / the
  changes after the work (and how we will measure them) / the risks of the
  work itself.
- Propose increments that ship together with feature work. A manager will
  decline a proposal that needs a feature freeze. If a freeze is truly the
  only way, say so.
- Give the strongest honest version of the "leave it alone" position. Then
  answer it.
- Use one page maximum. The reader is a manager who chooses between this
  work and features.

My notes (the pain, the history, any numbers):
<<<paste complaints, incident links, and examples of slow features>>>
```
